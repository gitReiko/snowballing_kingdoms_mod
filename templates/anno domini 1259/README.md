
## Summary

Spawns new clans for kingdoms that conquer new settlements. This allows you to play for your favorite kingdom or create your own. Just capture settlements. Other kingdoms are also strengthening (this creates challenge even at late stage).

## When possible enable/disable mod?

At any stage of the game.

If you enable mod, new clans will be created. Probably a lot of them in a short period of time, if conditions are met.

If turned off, no new clans will be created. But created clans will remain with you until the end of the game.

## Compatibility with Erik's Troops 

Full compatibility. You can load snowball earlier or later. Should work.

## Will this mod work with other mods?

Yes, but will create clans inappropriate for cultures since other mods have other id and other cultures.

You will also need to enable the BannerPaste mod to display banners correctly. And set "preventNPCBannerColorChanges": true .

## Historicity

This mod is not historical. Rather based on history and archaeology.

There is no single timeline. However, the main focus is on the middle ages. Different cultures may use banners from different centuries.

I did not intend to offend anyone with any of designs or names. I know nothing about families and their banners. I just take collections from public sources. The grouping by cultures is approximate and rough. I hope nothing here is inappropriate or offensive — the intention was opposite.
